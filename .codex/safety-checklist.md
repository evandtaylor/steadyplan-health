# Safety Checklist

Use this before shipping copy, forms, templates, beta flows, or generated plan content.

- No diagnosis.
- No treatment instructions.
- No prescribing.
- No peptide recommendations.
- No research chemical recommendations.
- No medication change advice.
- No disease cure claims.
- No supplement claims that imply treatment or disease prevention.
- No specific dosages for medications, peptides, research chemicals, or supplements.
- No unnecessary sensitive data collection.
- No requests for diagnoses, prescription medication lists, lab values, uploaded medical records, insurance numbers, medical record numbers, dates of birth, or Social Security numbers in the MVP.
- Required disclaimer is present where appropriate.
- Emergency language is present where appropriate and says to call 911 or seek emergency care.
- Outputs are framed as organization, planning, education, reminders, checklists, routines, or questions to ask a qualified professional.
- SuppPlan language does not tell users what to take.
- SuppPlan language does not claim to detect interactions.
- Nutrition language does not provide disease-specific medical nutrition therapy.
