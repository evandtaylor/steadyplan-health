# ShiftPlan AI Photoshoot Prompt Pack

Use these prompts for Pomelli or other AI image tools when creating website, campaign, and social visuals. Keep images grounded, modern, and practical. ShiftPlan should feel like a calm planning tool for people with hard schedules, not a generic healthcare brand.

## General Brand Image Prompt

Create a realistic lifestyle image for ShiftPlan, a private beta weekly planning app for people with irregular work schedules. Show a modern phone or laptop on a dark desk with a simple weekly schedule, checklist, and coffee or water nearby. The mood should be calm, focused, and useful. Use dark navy and black tones with subtle teal and blue accents. Avoid fake readable app text, medical imagery, and stock-photo smiles. The image should feel premium, practical, and mobile-app focused.

## Homepage Hero Prompt

Create a cinematic but realistic homepage hero image for ShiftPlan. Show a phone in hand with a clean dark-mode weekly planning interface, soft evening light, and a subtle sense of a real work schedule being organized. The image should immediately communicate "turn your schedule into a realistic weekly plan." Use dark navy, black, teal, and blue accents. Keep the background simple and uncluttered. No visible fake text, no medical equipment, no hospital scene, no generic stock look.

## 7a-7p Campaign Image Prompt

Create a realistic campaign image for a 7a-7p shift worker planning the week. Show a phone with a dark-mode weekly plan, a simple packed lunch, keys, and a work bag on a kitchen counter in early morning light. The mood should be practical and calm, not dramatic. Use dark neutral tones with teal/blue app accents. Do not show medical treatment, workplace safety scenes, or fake readable text.

## 7p-7a Campaign Image Prompt

Create a realistic campaign image for a 7p-7a night-shift worker organizing the week. Show a phone with a dark-mode timeline plan next to a notebook, water bottle, and low warm lighting. The setting should feel like late afternoon or evening before work. Keep it grounded and respectful. Use dark navy/black with teal-blue highlights. Avoid hospital imagery, sleep-treatment claims, fake text, and generic exhausted stereotypes.

## Off-Day Reset Image Prompt

Create a calm lifestyle image showing an off-day planning moment. A phone displays a simple dark-mode checklist on a table with groceries, a laundry basket in the background, and soft natural light. The feeling should be "light first off day" and realistic routine planning. No medical claims, no therapy vibe, no fake readable app text. Use modern dark tones with small teal/blue accents.

## Meal Prep Planning Image Prompt

Create a practical meal prep planning image for ShiftPlan. Show a phone with a dark-mode weekly checklist next to simple containers of food, a grocery list, and a kitchen counter. The scene should feel doable, not perfect or influencer-staged. Use realistic lighting, dark neutral surfaces, and subtle teal-blue app accents. Avoid diet claims, supplement imagery, medical claims, and fake readable text.

## Private Beta Image Prompt

Create a premium private beta visual for ShiftPlan. Show a dark-mode app invitation or access-code-style screen on a phone, with a weekly planning checklist hinted in the background. The image should feel exclusive but friendly, like a small private beta for real shift schedules. Use dark navy, black, teal, and blue. Avoid visible fake text, hype-heavy startup imagery, or medical environments.

## Social Swipe-Video Background Prompt

Create a clean background for short-form swipe videos about shift-work weekly planning. Use a dark navy/black gradient-free background with subtle desk texture, a faint phone silhouette, and teal/blue accent lines. Leave plenty of empty space for large white text overlays. The image should be legible on mobile and not visually busy. Avoid stock-photo people, fake text, and medical imagery.

## Phone-In-Hand App Image Prompt

Create a realistic close-up image of a person holding a phone with a dark-mode weekly planning app interface. The screen should suggest cards, timeline blocks, and a checklist without readable fake text. The environment should feel like home before or after a shift, with practical items nearby such as keys, lunch bag, notebook, or water bottle. Use dark navy/black with teal/blue accents. Hands should look natural and undistorted.

## Website Section Image Prompt

Create a realistic product-section image for ShiftPlan showing a laptop and phone side by side, both using a dark-mode weekly planning interface. The phone should show a checklist-style view and the laptop should show a weekly timeline. The image should feel like a real app workflow, not a marketing mockup. Use clean composition, dark surfaces, teal/blue accents, and no readable fake text.

## What To Avoid

- Cheesy healthcare imagery.
- Visible fake text.
- Distorted hands.
- Overly medical environments.
- Generic stock-photo look.
- White-coat or hospital-centered visuals.
- Exhausted-worker stereotypes.
- Medication, supplement, or treatment imagery.
- Busy backgrounds behind future text overlays.
- Claims implied through imagery that ShiftPlan treats health conditions.

## Prompt Review Checklist

Before using a generated image, check:

- Does it look like a real weekly planning product?
- Is it clearly lifestyle/routine planning, not healthcare?
- Are hands, phones, and screens credible?
- Is there enough space for website or social text?
- Are any visible words fake, misspelled, or misleading?
- Does the image avoid medical and treatment implications?
