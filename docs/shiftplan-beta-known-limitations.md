# ShiftPlan Beta Known Limitations

Use this when inviting or supporting private beta testers.

## What Testers Should Know

- ShiftPlan plans are AI-generated drafts.
- Review dates, shift times, appointments, errands, and assumptions before using a plan.
- Calendar export is `.ics` download only. It is not Google Calendar or Apple Calendar sync.
- Calendar exports do not add automatic reminders.
- Interactive checklist completion is saved on the current device only.
- Beta access uses email plus access code.
- There is no public account creation yet.
- There is no native iPhone app yet.
- There are no push notifications yet.
- ShiftPlan is for lifestyle and routine planning only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, fatigue treatment, burnout treatment, sleep disorder guidance, medication guidance, supplement guidance, healthcare guidance, mental health guidance, workplace safety guidance, or emergency support.

## How To Report Issues

Ask testers to send:

- What they were trying to do.
- Device and browser.
- Screenshot if the issue is visual.
- The week start date they used, if relevant.
- Whether the issue happened before or after generating a plan.
- Whether the issue blocked them or was just confusing.

Avoid asking testers to send sensitive health, medication, diagnosis, emergency, workplace safety, or private workplace details.
