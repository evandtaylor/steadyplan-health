# ShiftPlan Content Strategy

ShiftPlan content should make shift workers feel seen quickly. The best format for early testing is short, faceless swipe-style videos that show a messy schedule becoming a realistic weekly plan.

## Content Pillars

- 7a-7p schedule breakdowns
- 7p-7a schedule breakdowns
- 3x12 cheat codes
- Mistakes that ruin off days
- Comment your schedule
- Build in public

## Repeatable 9-Slide Video Structure

1. Hook
2. Pain point
3. Example schedule
4. Mistake
5. Better plan
6. Placement
7. Checklist
8. ShiftPlan angle
9. CTA

## Example 9-Slide Outline

1. "Working 3x12s this week?"
2. "The hard part is not the shifts. It is fitting life around them."
3. "Example: Mon/Tue/Wed 7a-7p."
4. "Mistake: errands after 12-hour shifts."
5. "Better plan: prep before the stretch, batch errands Friday."
6. "Workouts go short and realistic, not heroic."
7. "Checklist: meals, laundry, gas, family dinner, reset."
8. "ShiftPlan turns your exact schedule into a weekly plan."
9. "Comment SHIFT or join beta at shiftplan.ai."

## Design Direction

- Dark navy background
- Teal/blue glow accents
- Big white text
- Simple schedule blocks
- App mockup cards
- Checklist cards
- Minimal motion
- No hospital portal feel
- No medical/treatment claims

## Safety and Positioning

- Keep the promise focused on lifestyle and routine planning.
- Do not claim to fix fatigue, burnout, sleep, anxiety, or medical conditions.
- Do not discuss diagnoses, medications, symptoms, or protected health information.
- Talk about schedules, meals, workouts, errands, appointments, responsibilities, reset blocks, and realistic planning.

## Build-in-Public Angles

- Show the app getting better from tester feedback.
- Share before/after examples of plan output becoming more human.
- Ask nurses and shift workers which features should come next.
- Be honest that ShiftPlan is private beta and still learning.
