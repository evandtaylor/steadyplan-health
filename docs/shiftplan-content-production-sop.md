# ShiftPlan Content Production SOP

Use this SOP to produce repeatable faceless short-form content for ShiftPlan without drifting into medical, treatment, or workplace-safety claims. The content goal is simple: show shift workers that ShiftPlan helps turn a real schedule into a realistic weekly routine.

## Weekly Content Workflow

1. Pick 3-5 topics from the content pillars.
2. Write hooks that name a specific schedule or planning problem.
3. Draft each post as a 7-9 slide swipe video.
4. Keep each slide to one clear idea.
5. Use a simple app/checklist visual, schedule example, or planning prompt.
6. Add one clear CTA.
7. Review for prohibited claims.
8. Batch create assets.
9. Schedule or post manually.
10. Track comments, saves, DMs, beta signups, and repeated requests.

## How To Choose Topics

Prioritize topics that:

- Match real beta tester feedback.
- Show a specific schedule, not a vague productivity idea.
- Reduce planning friction for the week.
- Invite comments with actual schedules.
- Connect naturally to the private beta.

Good topic patterns:

- "If you work 7a-7p, plan your week like this."
- "If your first off day disappears, try this routine structure."
- "Comment your schedule and I will show how ShiftPlan would map the week."
- "What to put in your weekly request so the plan is actually useful."

Avoid topics that:

- Promise to treat fatigue, burnout, sleep disorders, anxiety, or health conditions.
- Give medical, medication, supplement, workplace safety, or emergency advice.
- Claim a plan will fix night shift life.
- Shame people for messy weeks.

## How To Make 7-9 Slide Swipe Videos

Use this structure:

1. Hook: name the schedule or problem.
2. Context: why the week is hard to plan.
3. Constraint: work stretch, commute, errands, meals, or first off day.
4. ShiftPlan angle: what the app considers.
5. Example day plan: show a small timeline.
6. Checklist: show 3 realistic actions.
7. Reuse: show saved defaults or "what changed this week."
8. CTA: comment, join beta, or log in if invited.
9. Optional reminder: lifestyle planning only.

Keep slides short. A viewer should understand each slide in under two seconds.

## Visual Style

- Dark navy or black base.
- Teal/blue accents.
- Big white text.
- One idea per slide.
- App/checklist mockups.
- Realistic weekly schedule snippets.
- Minimal decorative clutter.
- High contrast for mobile viewing.

Avoid:

- Generic stock-photo energy.
- Busy backgrounds behind small text.
- Fake medical imagery.
- Too many colors.
- Long paragraphs.
- Tiny app screenshots that cannot be read.

## Content Pillars

### 7a-7p

- Before-work routines.
- Commute-aware planning.
- Simple meals around long days.
- Light first off day.
- Planning errands away from workdays.

### 7p-7a

- Night-shift week structure.
- First day off reset.
- Low-friction meals.
- Keeping obligations grouped.
- Planning without pretending the week is normal.

### 3x12 Cheat Codes

- Protecting the first off day.
- Batch errands.
- Weekly prep windows.
- Short workouts on realistic days.
- Using saved defaults to avoid retyping.

### Off-Day Planning

- Light first off day.
- One anchor task per day.
- Errands on one block.
- Flexible meal prep.
- Recovery-style routine language without treatment claims.

### Night Shift

- Timeline examples.
- Work block visibility.
- Reset block language.
- Meal and chore planning.
- Weekly request examples.

### Comment Your Schedule

- Ask users to comment a schedule.
- Show a sample ShiftPlan structure.
- Invite beta users to test the app.
- Use comments to identify repeated schedule patterns.

## CTA Rules

Use clear, low-pressure CTAs:

- "Comment SHIFT."
- "Comment NIGHTS."
- "Comment your schedule."
- "Join the private beta at shiftplan.ai."
- "Have beta access? Log in at shiftplan.ai/app."
- "Tell ShiftPlan what changed this week."

Avoid:

- "Fix your fatigue."
- "Cure burnout."
- "Sleep better guaranteed."
- "Never feel exhausted again."
- "Medical-grade planning."

## What Not To Say

Do not make:

- Medical claims.
- Treatment claims.
- Burnout treatment claims.
- Fatigue treatment claims.
- Sleep disorder claims.
- Medication or supplement guidance.
- Healthcare guidance.
- Mental health guidance.
- Workplace safety guidance.
- Emergency-support guidance.

ShiftPlan positioning should stay: lifestyle/routine planning for people with hard schedules.

## Simple Weekly Posting Schedule

| Day | Post type | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | 7a-7p swipe video | Reach day-shift workers with a concrete schedule example |
| Tuesday | Comment your schedule post | Collect real schedules and content prompts |
| Wednesday | 7p-7a swipe video | Reach night-shift workers with a practical weekly structure |
| Thursday | 3x12 cheat codes post | Show the product's scheduling point of view |
| Friday | Beta CTA / app preview | Invite the next small batch of testers |
| Saturday | Off-day planning post | Show useful lifestyle/routine planning |
| Sunday | Weekly request example | Teach people how to tell ShiftPlan what changed |

## Weekly Review

At the end of each week, record:

- Which posts got comments with real schedules.
- Which hooks got saves or shares.
- Which CTAs led to beta interest.
- Which questions appeared more than once.
- Which idea should become a product or prompt improvement.
