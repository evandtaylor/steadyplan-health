# ShiftPlan Private Beta Follow-Up Messages

Copy/paste messages for following up with private beta testers. Keep the tone casual, specific, and low-pressure.

## First Login, No Plan Generated

Hey [name], I saw you got into ShiftPlan. No rush, but when you have 3-5 minutes, try creating one week from your real schedule. The most helpful test is your actual shifts plus whatever changed this week.

If anything feels confusing before you generate a plan, send me a screenshot or just tell me where you got stuck.

## Plan Generated, No Feedback Yet

Hey [name], thanks for trying ShiftPlan. When you get a second, can you open the saved plan and leave quick feedback at the bottom?

The big things I am looking for:
- Did the plan feel realistic?
- Was anything too robotic or too much to read?
- Would you actually use the checklist during the week?

Short notes are perfect.

## Feedback Submitted

Thank you for leaving feedback. That is exactly the kind of real-world signal I need right now.

I am using beta notes to make ShiftPlan faster, less scroll-heavy, and better at turning messy schedules into something usable. If you try another week, I would love to know whether the next plan feels closer.

## Would Use Weekly / Would Pay

Quick follow-up: based on the plan you got, would you use something like this every week if it stayed simple?

Also, gut check: would $9/month feel fair for a polished version, or would it need to do more first?

No need to soften the answer. Honest is more useful than nice here.

## Feature Ranking

I am deciding what to build next for ShiftPlan. Can you rank these from most useful to least useful for you?

- Faster weekly request form
- Better daily timeline
- Smarter saved preferences
- Calendar export improvements
- Checklist improvements
- Reuse last week and update only what changed
- More human wording

If one of these is not useful at all, mark it that way too.
